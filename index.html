Decision engine

Most money tools show the past. PAM shows what happens next.

PAM is not a budgeting tracker or spreadsheet. It models choices like rent, cars, trips, job changes, taxes, investing, and moving out.

Example

Can I buy a car with a $400/month payment?

  • Monthly impact: -$400
  • Risk: Medium
  • Goal impact: moving out may be delayed by 6-9 months
  • Explanation: affordable, but your monthly buffer gets tighter
